# Founder Notes - Valora AI (Bengaluru-First)

Updated: March 2026
Audience: Founder and leadership team
Purpose: Operating playbook for execution, fundraising narrative discipline, and weekly decision-making

---

## 1. Founder Narrative (Always Consistent)

Use this exact framing in investor, customer, and hiring conversations:

- Valora is a self-learning GIS AI decision agent for real estate, starting in Bengaluru.
- We are not a listings portal and not a generic chatbot.
- Our wedge is broker productivity with explainable, evidence-backed recommendations.
- Our model is hybrid: subscription + usage + B2B pilots.
- Our north-star metric is Weekly Active Workflow Users (WAWU).

If a conversation drifts, reset to:
1. Decision quality problem
2. Product proof
3. Monetization quality
4. 24-month execution path

## 3. Founder Operating Cadence

### Weekly (Non-Negotiable)
1. Monday: KPI review (WAWU, conversion, retention, top-up attach, pilot pipeline)
2. Tuesday: Customer problem review (broker calls, churn calls, win/loss insights)
3. Wednesday: Product and reliability review (feature quality, defects, data freshness)
4. Thursday: GTM review (pipeline health, channel quality, deal blockers)
5. Friday: Risk review and next-week commitments

### Monthly (Board/Investor Ready)
1. Revenue quality report (subscription vs usage vs B2B mix)
2. Cohort retention deep dive
3. Top-12 market coverage confidence and freshness report
4. Risk register status with mitigation progress

## 5. Founder GTM Notes (Brokers First)

### Broker Acquisition Channels
1. Direct founder-led outreach in top-12 micro-markets
2. Association and referral network channels
3. Power-user advocates and local case stories

### Broker Onboarding Script
1. Define target client profile
2. Configure one live workflow (alert + follow-up task + lead)
3. Produce one client-ready report in first session
4. Track first 7-day usage and intervene fast if inactive

### Pilot Success Definition
- Success is not sign-up volume.
- Success is recurring workflow usage and client-facing output generation.

## 6. Founder Product Notes (Build Discipline)

### Build What Improves WAWU
- Prioritize reliability and recurring workflow utility over feature novelty.

### Explainability Is Commercial
- If users cannot defend recommendations, retention drops.
- Always include confidence score, risk flags, and evidence traces.

### Learning Loop Must Be Explicit
- Every recommendation interaction should feed measurable learning signals.

## 7. Founder Fundraising Notes

### What to Emphasize
1. Bengaluru-first constrained strategy (quality before expansion)
2. Product proof already live
3. Clear monetization and retention mechanics
4. Execution risk awareness and mitigation plan

### What to Avoid
1. Unverified market claims without dated citations
2. City-expansion promises without operational readiness
3. Vanity metrics replacing retention or revenue quality
4. Conflicting pricing/tier statements across materials

---

## 8. Founder Risk Memo

Top risks and response posture:

1. Data freshness drift
- Response: freshness SLAs, confidence bands, UI timestamp disclosure

2. Billing/tiering inconsistency
- Response: single pricing source-of-truth and contract validation

3. Trust/explainability gaps
- Response: evidence-first outputs and post-response verification checks

4. GTM concentration risk
- Response: channel diversification and phased segment expansion

---

## 9. Founder Decision Checklist (Before Any New Initiative)

1. Does this improve WAWU or retention quality?
2. Does this reduce time-to-first-value for brokers?
3. Does this strengthen monetization consistency?
4. Does this improve explainability and trust?
5. Can this be measured in 30 days with clear owner accountability?

If the answer is no to 3 or more items, defer.
